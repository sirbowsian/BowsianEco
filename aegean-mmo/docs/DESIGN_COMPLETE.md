# 🏛️ AEGEAN: OATHS OF OLYMPUS
## Complete Game Design Document

**Tagline:** *Sail the wine-dark sea. Earn the favor of gods. Become the myth they will never forget.*

---

## 1. HIGH CONCEPT

**Aegean: Oaths of Olympus** is a massive open-world Greek mythology MMO where players become mortal heroes chosen by the gods. You sail between islands, fight mythic beasts, train dozens of skills, build your own estate, join poleis, earn divine favor, and shape the fate of the Aegean.

### Inspiration Blend
- **RuneScape**: Deep skilling, quests, crafting, economy, repeatable content, long-term goals
- **Assassin's Creed Odyssey**: Lush Greek islands, naval sailing, hoplite combat, temples, statues, oracles, mercenaries, mythic grandeur
- **MMO Systems**: Player cities, trade, factions, raids, world events, naval warfare, god-driven seasonal updates

### Core Fantasy
You are not just a warrior. You are a hero of legend.

A player might:
- Hunt the Minotaur in a shifting labyrinth
- Sail a trireme through a storm sent by Poseidon
- Train as a blacksmith and forge legendary armor for their polis
- Become a priest of Apollo and bless armies before battle
- Raid the Underworld with a party of five
- Own a vineyard on Naxos and export wine to other players
- Choose to honor or betray the gods

**The fantasy is: "Live a myth, not just play one."**

## 5. COMBAT DESIGN

### Style
- Real-time action combat
- Light attack, heavy attack, dodge, block/parry
- Skills and abilities on cooldown
- No hard tab-target only, but optional assist targeting

### Weapon Types

| Weapon | Style | Specialty |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Sword and Shield | Balanced | Defensive, versatile |
| Spear | Reach | Thrust attacks, phalanx synergy |
| Dagger | Fast | Stealth, critical hits |
| Bow | Ranged | Precision, hunting |
| Javelin | Ranged Burst | High damage, hunting |
| Staff / Ritual Blade | Support | Divine magic, debuffs |
| Hammer / Axe | Heavy | Armor break, high damage |

### Hero Abilities (Arete Skills)
Players equip a limited set of abilities:

- **Aegis Guard**: Block all frontal damage for 2 seconds
- **Spartan Kick**: Knock enemies back
- **Apollo's Radiance**: Blind and burn enemies
- **Hermes Step**: Short dash with invulnerability frames
- **Poseidon's Pull**: Pull enemies toward you like a tide
- **Artemis Shot**: Charged piercing arrow
- **Ares Frenzy**: Damage increases as health drops
- **Athena's Insight**: Reveal enemy weaknesses

## 6. DIVINE SYSTEM

The gods are not passive quest givers. They are active forces.

### 6.1 Divine Favor
Each god has a reputation path. Favor earned by:
- Completing quests
- Making sacrifices
- Participating in festivals
- Building temples
- Helping followers
- Smiting enemies of the god
- Moral choices

### 6.2 God Blessings

#### Zeus
- Chain lightning on heavy attacks
- Storm immunity while sailing

#### Athena
- Reduced cooldowns when outnumbered
- Reveal hidden tactics in PvP

#### Poseidon
- Better ship speed
- Summon wave knockback

#### Apollo
- Healing sunlight aura
- Bonus damage to undead

#### Artemis
- Track enemies on minimap
- Bonus ranged damage

#### Ares
- Damage increase during combat streaks
- Fear nearby enemies at low health

#### Aphrodite
- Charm NPCs temporarily
- Reduce enemy aggression

#### Hermes
- Faster travel and dodge
- Better courier mission rewards

#### Hephaestus
- Better smithing success chance
- Repair gear in the field

#### Demeter
- Harvest bonuses
- Healing from crops

#### Dionysus
- Chaotic buffs at festivals
- Wine-based status effects

#### Hades
- Resist death once per day
- Command shades temporarily

#### Hera
- Marriage and oath bonuses
- Political influence boost

#### Hestia
- Hearthstone-like travel
- Housing production bonuses

### 6.3 Hubris Meter

If players act arrogantly or defy the gods too much, they gain **Hubris**.

**High Hubris Consequences:**
- Monsters target you more aggressively
- Priests refuse service
- Divine punishments (lightning strikes, curses)
- Nemesis agents hunt you
- Some quests close off

**High Hubris Benefits:**
- Forbidden powers unlock
- Titan-related quests available
- Anti-god gear accessible
- Secret endings achievable

**This creates meaningful roleplay:** Pious hero, tragic rebel, or hubristic tyrant.

## 24. EXAMPLE GAMEPLAY LOOP

### Core Loop
1. **Take quest** from city notice board, god shrine, or NPC
2. **Travel** by foot, horse, or ship to destination
3. **Engage** in combat, investigation, crafting, or negotiation
4. **Gain** skill experience, resources, and reputation
5. **Return** to city to trade, upgrade gear, socialize
6. **Join** group content: dungeon, boss, festival, or raid
7. **Increase** divine favor and faction reputation
8. **Unlock** new islands, gear, ships, and mythic content
9. **Repeat** with escalating stakes and rewards

### Daily Play Session (1-2 hours)
- Complete 2-3 daily rites

## 27. MONETIZATION STRATEGY

**Philosophy:** Avoid pay-to-win. Maintain game integrity.

### Acceptable Monetization

#### Cosmetics
- Armor dyes (unique colors)
- Ship sails (custom designs)
- Figureheads (decorative prows)
- Housing decorations (furniture, murals)
- Mount skins (special horses, dolphins)
- Festival outfits (seasonal costumes)
- Weapon appearances (visual only)
- Emotes (dances, gestures)

#### Convenience (Carefully Balanced)
- Premium name changes
- Server transfer (once per month)
- Extra bank tabs (minor QoL)
- Character slot expansion

#### Content
- Battle pass with cosmetics and minor boosters (no gear)
- Expansion packs (new regions, storylines)
- Collector's editions (cosmetic bundles)

### STRICTLY AVOID
- Selling endgame gear
- Pay-to-win naval ships
- Direct sale of powerful blessings
- Pay-to-skip major progression
- Loot boxes with gameplay advantages
- Energy systems limiting playtime
